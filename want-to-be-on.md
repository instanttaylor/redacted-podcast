# Want to be on [Redacted]?

[Redacted] is about the **messy middle** of building with AI. Most "how I AI" content is polished — clean demos, perfect workflows, everything works on the first try. This show is the opposite: we show the rebuilds, the dead ends, the stuff that broke, and the steps everyone else edits out.

If you're using AI in genuinely interesting ways to automate or run your business, we want to talk to you.

Two things matter more than the grid:

1. **You're doing something genuinely interesting with AI** — automating or running real parts of the business, not just trying a tool.
2. **You're up for showing real work.** Anything private stays private — we keep the sensitive stuff out through editing (that's the whole [redacted] premise), so you can share freely.

## What being on the show looks like

- **15–20 minutes to show us something** you're actually doing — screen share, walk us through it.
- Then **a conversation about it** — what works, what broke, what you'd do differently.
- Recorded on **StreamYard**.
- Booked **at least a couple weeks in advance**
- Taylor will reach out and ask some questions + find a good timeslot + book if it's a good fit.
