Just the two of us for the first time in over a month, and back to the show's roots: not "here's the thing I built" but why it got built and how it came together. Two halves of one product loop — a prototype and the AI-led feedback form made to test it — plus a bonus site rebuild that took ten turns.

## What we cover

- **"Peeling that onion back"** — the drift toward polished demos, and the correction: here's why we built it and here's how it links to something we actually care about
- **The Hot List** — an opening-soon / recently-opened feed for Raleigh, something Offline used to cover by hand and could never bring back before AI
- **Watching Instagram at scale** — a $140/month RapidAPI social API lets the pipeline do what a human would (follow every venue and news account in the city), and an LLM turns the reels it sees into stories
- **Borrowing your own app's skeleton** — instead of ShadCN and a fresh component library, Claude read the Offline Flutter repo and recreated the app's home screen around the new data
- **"How would you even do this in Figma?"** — the argument for a semi-production prototype on localhost when the whole point of the feature is live, non-stale data
- **The feedback form Typeform can't build** — video intro, the prototype embedded in the page, a few direct questions, then LLM-led follow-ups running on a Qwen model on Cloudflare, capped at three interview turns
- **"It's Typeform if an LLM typed the questions"** — the read on Formless, and why waiting twelve to eighteen months for a SaaS company to ship what you want is the wrong trade
- **Build the API for the LLM, not the form for yourself** — every new feature also gets a backend the model can drive, so a survey gets made from a chat instead of by filling in fields and clicking save
- **North star and behavioral linkage** — RICE and *Evidence Guided* against ~3,600 RDU subscribers: before asking whether a feature helps, ask whether the inactive cohort is going out to eat at all
- **Hire an agent to watch the numbers** — the piece of the loop that doesn't exist yet: something holding the survey and research context that reports back whether the launched feature moved the metric
- **Two months, then one month, then two weeks** — the compounding case for eventually running five parallel tests against five populations of users
- **Bonus: Webflow out, rebuilt in an hour** — "I am not going to put more than an hour into this project," ten turns, one big correction when the MCP dropped and the styles got invented, and an FAQ that had been wrong for nine months finally fixed
- **"A leak in the gas tank"** — Webflow shipping an MCP good enough to let you take everything and leave
