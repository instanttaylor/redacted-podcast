Ben Eckerson opens up the internal CRM he built for his agency: paste in a client call transcript, get back both the proposal you put in front of the client and the build sheet, hours, and margin you keep for yourself.

## What we cover

- "Getting the humans to stop doing the robot work" — the line Agentcy runs on
- The tool: built incrementally over six to eight months, Gemini underneath
- A hand-maintained JSON file of Agentcy's offerings and case studies grounds every output
- Lead in, pre-call research and questions out, then the transcript does the rest
- Two sides of the house: the client-facing framing vs. the internal stage signals, stack, unknowns, and risks
- The AI-drafted quote: phases, hours, confidence, break-even, and margin on contractors
- One-click proposal deck, editable inline, exportable as markdown to restyle in Claude
- Why he hasn't switched to an off-the-shelf AI CRM, and what would make him
- "The days of the SaaS platform are numbered" — tools tailored to one person's business
- Going headless: without a UI forcing structure, does the output quietly get sloppier?
- Decks don't land, prototypes do — building the thing so the client sees it
- Most clients have no CTO, just someone managing licenses, unaware they already have API access
