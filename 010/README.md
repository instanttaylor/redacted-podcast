Parker Mayes shows Signify, the signal layer he's building between data providers and your CRM, and the experimental agent pipeline that runs his LinkedIn outreach while he goes to the gym.

## What we cover

- Signify: sits between Apollo/ZoomInfo and your CRM, surfaces who to reach out to today
- Scraping + scoring 100 companies a day for a couple bucks
- The four daily agents: scan → connect → add to pipeline → messages 2 and 3
- "It needs to document its own process" — agents writing their own SOPs
- Humans do human things: personalized selfie videos, never automating warm leads
- Teaching the agent Parker's voice (lowercase heys, "lmk", template guardrails)
- Three concurrent browser tabs running personalized demo screenshots
- Restaurants vs. B2B SaaS: the tangled context web problem
- "Nice cold outreach, BTW" — when prospects compliment the AI's DMs
- Sales co-founder + cracked developer, no technical co-founder
- "It'll take you a day, not months"
